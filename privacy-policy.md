# RK Sports Payroll Automation Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

RK Sports Payroll Automation is an internal business application used by RK Sports, Inc. for payroll processing, payroll review, payroll preparation, and accounting operations.

The application accesses QuickBooks Online information solely for authorized payroll and accounting purposes.

No customer information is sold, rented, licensed, transferred, or disclosed to third parties except as required by law.

Data accessed through QuickBooks Online is used exclusively by RK Sports, Inc. employees for internal business operations.

The application does not provide public access to QuickBooks Online data.
